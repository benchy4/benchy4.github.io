# Validation report

- Reconstructed 1216 paired team-game rows from 608 games.
- Threshold candidates are limited to the central 10th–90th percentile range and natural metric increments.
- Selection requires at least 40 discovery and 25 validation observations, with no more than 80% overall coverage.
- Wilson intervals and Benjamini–Hochberg q-values are supplied.
- Earlier four seasons are used for threshold discovery; later seasons are held out for validation.
- Combination rules require at least 40 discovery and 20 validation observations.
- Results are observational. Opponent quality, roster strength and game context remain possible confounders.
