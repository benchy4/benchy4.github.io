# Winning threshold findings

- Complete games: 619
- Team-game observations: 1238
- Discovery seasons: 2019–20 through 2022–23
- Validation seasons: 2023–24 through 2026–27 (in progress)

## Strongest held-out single-stat thresholds

- Net rating at least 10.000: 100.0% wins in 208 validation team-games (95% CI 98.2%–100.0%).
- Offensive rating at least 105.000: 98.5% wins in 66 validation team-games (95% CI 91.9%–99.7%).
- Points allowed at most 65.000: 94.0% wins in 84 validation team-games (95% CI 86.8%–97.4%).
- Points scored at least 95.000: 93.8% wins in 65 validation team-games (95% CI 85.2%–97.6%).
- Defensive rating at most 70.000: 93.7% wins in 63 validation team-games (95% CI 84.8%–97.5%).
- eFG% at least 0.575: 92.0% wins in 87 validation team-games (95% CI 84.3%–96.0%).
- FG% at least 0.500: 90.3% wins in 93 validation team-games (95% CI 82.6%–94.8%).
- 3P% at least 0.450: 89.2% wins in 65 validation team-games (95% CI 79.4%–94.7%).
- 2P% at least 0.575: 82.7% wins in 98 validation team-games (95% CI 74.0%–88.9%).
- Kills at least 10.000: 82.1% wins in 112 validation team-games (95% CI 74.0%–88.1%).
- Assists at least 22.000: 81.3% wins in 91 validation team-games (95% CI 72.1%–88.0%).
- Assist/turnover ratio at least 2.000: 78.7% wins in 75 validation team-games (95% CI 68.1%–86.4%).

These thresholds were selected on earlier seasons and evaluated on later seasons. They remain descriptive associations, not causal targets. Scoring and efficiency thresholds are especially close to the game outcome.
