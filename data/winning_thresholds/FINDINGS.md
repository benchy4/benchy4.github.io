# Winning threshold findings

- Complete games: 608
- Team-game observations: 1216
- Discovery seasons: 2019–20 through 2022–23
- Validation seasons: 2023–24 through 2025–26

## Strongest held-out single-stat thresholds

- Net rating at least 10.000: 100.0% wins in 198 validation team-games (95% CI 98.1%–100.0%).
- Offensive rating at least 105.000: 98.3% wins in 60 validation team-games (95% CI 91.1%–99.7%).
- Points allowed at most 65.000: 94.0% wins in 84 validation team-games (95% CI 86.8%–97.4%).
- Defensive rating at most 70.000: 93.7% wins in 63 validation team-games (95% CI 84.8%–97.5%).
- Points scored at least 95.000: 93.2% wins in 59 validation team-games (95% CI 83.8%–97.3%).
- 3P% at least 0.450: 91.8% wins in 61 validation team-games (95% CI 82.2%–96.4%).
- eFG% at least 0.575: 91.6% wins in 83 validation team-games (95% CI 83.6%–95.9%).
- FG% at least 0.500: 89.9% wins in 89 validation team-games (95% CI 81.9%–94.6%).
- Kills at least 10.000: 82.1% wins in 112 validation team-games (95% CI 74.0%–88.1%).
- 2P% at least 0.575: 81.5% wins in 92 validation team-games (95% CI 72.4%–88.1%).
- Assists at least 22.000: 81.4% wins in 86 validation team-games (95% CI 71.9%–88.2%).
- Free throw rate (FTA/FGA) at least 0.350: 78.6% wins in 98 validation team-games (95% CI 69.5%–85.5%).

These thresholds were selected on earlier seasons and evaluated on later seasons. They remain descriptive associations, not causal targets. Scoring and efficiency thresholds are especially close to the game outcome.
