# Winning threshold findings

- Complete games: 623
- Team-game observations: 1246
- Discovery seasons: 2019–20 through 2022–23
- Validation seasons: 2023–24 through 2026–27 (in progress)

## Strongest held-out single-stat thresholds

- Net rating at least 10.000: 100.0% wins in 210 validation team-games (95% CI 98.2%–100.0%).
- Offensive rating at least 105.000: 98.5% wins in 67 validation team-games (95% CI 92.0%–99.7%).
- Points scored at least 95.000: 93.8% wins in 65 validation team-games (95% CI 85.2%–97.6%).
- Defensive rating at most 70.000: 93.7% wins in 63 validation team-games (95% CI 84.8%–97.5%).
- Points allowed at most 65.000: 93.0% wins in 86 validation team-games (95% CI 85.6%–96.8%).
- eFG% at least 0.575: 92.0% wins in 88 validation team-games (95% CI 84.5%–96.1%).
- FG% at least 0.500: 90.4% wins in 94 validation team-games (95% CI 82.8%–94.9%).
- 3P% at least 0.450: 88.1% wins in 67 validation team-games (95% CI 78.2%–93.8%).
- Kills at least 10.000: 82.3% wins in 113 validation team-games (95% CI 74.2%–88.2%).
- 2P% at least 0.575: 81.8% wins in 99 validation team-games (95% CI 73.1%–88.2%).
- Assists at least 22.000: 81.5% wins in 92 validation team-games (95% CI 72.4%–88.1%).
- Assist/turnover ratio at least 2.000: 79.2% wins in 77 validation team-games (95% CI 68.9%–86.8%).

These thresholds were selected on earlier seasons and evaluated on later seasons. They remain descriptive associations, not causal targets. Scoring and efficiency thresholds are especially close to the game outcome.
