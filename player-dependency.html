Season variance

Result Variance

Compare the actual record with the record expected from scoring margin.

Expected win% estimates how often a team with the same season-long points scored and points allowed would normally win. Wins +/- compares actual wins with that estimate: positive means more wins than the scoring margin predicted; negative means fewer. 3P volatility measures how much 3P% changed from game to game; a higher number means less consistent shooting. This does not adjust for schedule strength.
Roster construction

Roster continuity and early results

How much of a team’s prior-season rotation returned, and how that team performed across its first five games.

Prior MIN retained is the share of the previous season’s team minutes played by players who appeared for the same team in the next season. It weights major rotation players more than fringe players. Early results are each team’s first five games by date. The adjusted figures account for the previous season’s average margin and differences between seasons; they remain observational.
