Definitions and source notes

Nightly shares rank players anew in each game and give each game equal weight. The league benchmark gives each selected team-game equal weight. The fixed top five are the same five players ranked by their total points over the selected stretch.

Availability and ties. PPG uses games played. A scoring tie leaves the nightly shares unchanged. Players tied on total points are listed alphabetically.

Source. InStat player box scores. Two 2026–27 Sydney player totals use the documented event logs to reconcile to the final score. Sydney–Adelaide, 18 October · Sydney–Southside, 8 November.
