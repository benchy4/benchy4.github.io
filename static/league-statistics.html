Average definition: counting statistics are aggregated across all team-seasons, then divided by team-games in per-game mode or by participating teams in per-season mode. Shooting percentages are recalculated from combined makes and attempts instead of averaging percentages. PiP uses event-coded layups, floaters, hooks, alley-oops and dunks because court coordinates are unavailable. %Bench treats the first five players listed in each source box score as starters; %Lead is recorded game time spent ahead. Rank 1 is best; lower values rank better for turnovers, fouls, points allowed and defensive efficiency.
