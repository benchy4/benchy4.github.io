How these comparisons work

Player and team totals come from their existing season profiles. Player stints across multiple teams in a season are combined. Games, wins and losses stay as totals in either display.

League seasons pool all team box scores. Per-game figures are per team per game (total divided by team appearances); the games-played row counts each match once. League totals include both teams. Shooting percentages use total makes and attempts, never an average of percentages.

PPP uses the same possession denominators as the team profiles. Rates and percentages stay unchanged when switching to totals. A dash means no attempts or unavailable data. With two entries, the difference is B minus A; percentage differences are percentage points (pp).
